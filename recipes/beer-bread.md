# Easy 3-Ingredient Beer Bread

**Two to Four Out the Door** · Quick breads

A simple no-knead quick bread made with self-rising flour. No yeast proofing required.

## Ingredients
- 3 cups self-rising flour
- 12 oz beer (one standard can or bottle)
- 3 tablespoons granulated sugar

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Grease a 9×5-inch loaf pan.
2. Stir flour, beer, and sugar together until just combined. Do not overmix.
3. Spread batter into the loaf pan.
4. Bake for 45–55 minutes, until golden and a tester inserted in the center comes out clean. If the top browns too quickly, loosely tent with foil.
5. Cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then transfer to a rack before slicing.

**Optional:** Brush with melted butter after baking (adds a fourth ingredient).
